Our Story

From a small kitchen table to a growing craft studio — here's how GooDaaY began.

🎨

Handmade, With Purpose

GooDaaY started as a passion project — turning raw materials into thoughtful, handmade pieces for the home and everyday life. What began as a hobby quickly grew into a small business built on craftsmanship, sustainability, and genuine care for every customer.

Today, every item we sell is still made by hand, in small batches, using materials chosen for their quality and low environmental impact.

Our Values

What Guides Our Craft

  • Quality: Every piece is checked by hand before it ships.
  • Sustainability: We favor natural, recycled, and low-impact materials.
  • Community: We support local artisans and small-batch production.
  • Authenticity: No mass production — just real, handmade goods.
